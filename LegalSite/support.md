# Support / 支持

## Common checks

1. Confirm the device meets the app's iOS or macOS deployment requirement.
2. For food-search issues, try the simplified Chinese name, English name, or an alias.
3. For Health data, open Apple Health and confirm permission plus Apple Watch synchronization.
4. For DeepSeek recognition, verify network access, API key, and account balance.
5. For iCloud synchronization, confirm both devices use the same Apple Account.

## Contact

Open an issue at https://github.com/lyonzhu/nutrition-diary-legal/issues and include the app version, device model, OS version, and steps to reproduce. Do not include API keys, personal health records, or meal photos containing private information.
